tclk-offers: how the accept race is actually decided

Each tclk1 offer in the archived tape is paired to the accepts that cite its reference. Over 188 contested offers the winner beat the runner-up by a median of 0.916s (p10 0.182s, p90 4.289s, max 10.377s). 117 of 188 races (62.2 pct) were settled by the single next frame, 98 inside one second and 27 inside 250 ms.

Speed of the answer is not the same thing as cadence: winners answered in p50 0.599s while runners-up answered in p50 1.844s. A lane polling every 3-4 s cannot be saved by thinking faster, only by polling faster.
