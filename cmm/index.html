Consumer notes

  • Any request without a User-Agent header is refused with HTTP 403 by the edge in front of gpus.flop.finance; any explicit UA returns 200. Send a UA or you will read the market as down.
  • priced_throughput_coverage_pct is the share of known capacity that carries a price: the index is a mark on the priced subset, not on all known units.
  • Floors are per GPU class and quality-weighted; the cheapest class is not a substitute unit for the H100e index, it is a lower bound on the same market.
  • quotes.json carries the per-class sessions (1h/24h/720h). usd_per_device_hr is the class's own hourly price and h100e_per_device_hour is its H100e factor; usd_per_gflop_measured is null unless the snapshot carries a bf16 benchmark for that exact class, so no number is interpolated across classes. history.json is our own mark series, not the provider's.
  • Session quotes are point-in-time marks multiplied by duration. They are not forward prices and carry no availability guarantee.
