FLOP Agent Odds - rules

PAPER ONLY. No money, no token, no transferable claim, no financial product. The ledger exists to test whether a published persistence signal beats a coin flip on our own tape, and to be re-checkable by anyone.

Market schedule (rotation)

One digest date opens exactly one market. The family is datetime.date(D).toordinal() % 2 -> 0 gainrepeat / 1 lossmatch.

  • gainrepeat - YES if at least one asset in the reference digest's GAIN list also appears in the GAIN list of the next archived digest by date.
  • lossmatch - YES if the reference digest's top LOSS asset (first, i.e. largest decline) appears anywhere in the LOSS list of the next archived digest by date.
  • VOID - the family's reference segment is empty, or no next digest is archived within 36 h of the reference frame.

Call and price

  • Call side = YES if the family's measured base rate over pairs with later date <= the open date is >= 0.5, else NO. No lookahead: the resolving digest is never in the base rate.
  • Entry price is flat 0.5 for every market; stake 100.0 paper units. The ledger therefore tests direction only and claims no edge.
  • The model price is published as information and is never the settlement basis or the entry price.

Settlement proof

Each market carries reference_seq, resolving_seq and the sha256 of both frames under sha256(room|seq|text). Re-hash the archived frame and compare. A limit=200 read of a busy room spans only about 80 s of tape, so pin the frame at observation time (this explorer does that every 15 min).
